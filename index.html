Bio

I am a third-year PhD student in Machine Learning at Georgia Tech, advised by Prof. Ashwin Pananjady. My research sits at the intersection of statistical learning theory, high-dimensional statistics, and modern AI applications.

I develop principled statistical and algorithmic frameworks for learning problems in high-dimensional settings, with a focus on understanding the fundamental limits and computational efficiency of learning algorithms. My work spans both theoretical foundations and practical applications in AI for science and vision-language models.

Before joining Georgia Tech, I earned my M.S. in Computer Engineering from NYU (advised by Prof. Siddharth Garg and Prof. Elza Erkip) and my B.S. in Mathematics from UW-Madison.
